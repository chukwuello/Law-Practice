## Drafting Rules

- **Numbered paragraphs** – one fact or point per paragraph where possible.
- **Active voice** – prefer “Defendant failed to pay” over “Payment was not made.”
- **Precise details** – use exact dates, amounts, names, and locations.
- **Defined terms** – define parties and key terms on first use.
- **No fabrication** – never invent facts, citations, or authorities.
- **Placeholders** – use `[ ]` for missing information.
- **Tone** – formal, respectful, and objective.
- **Citations** – every legal proposition should be supported by authority.
- **Local rules** – always defer to jurisdiction-specific requirements.
- **Facts, not evidence** – plead the event, not the proof.

---

## Citation & Verification Standards

- Use the citation format required by the court (e.g., Bluebook, ALWD, state-specific).
- Include pinpoint citations (e.g., `Id. at 457`).
- Verify that each case is still good law.
- Check statutes and regulations for amendments.
- Quote accurately; use ellipses and brackets properly.
- Flag any authority you cannot verify with `[VERIFY]`.

---

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Caption is correct and complete.
- [ ] All parties are properly named and identified.
- [ ] Jurisdiction and venue are adequately alleged.
- [ ] Facts are chronological, specific, and supported.
- [ ] **No evidence is pleaded** (no receipts, witness statements, or documents).
- [ ] Each claim or defense includes all required elements.
- [ ] Legal authority is cited and verified.
- [ ] Prayer for relief is specific and appropriate.
- [ ] Signature block is complete.
- [ ] Certificate of service is included if required.
- [ ] Formatting complies with local rules.
- [ ] No confidential information is improperly disclosed.
- [ ] Attorney review is noted before filing.

## Example Skeleton

**Caption:**
`IN THE HIGH COURT OF [STATE]`
`IN THE [JUDICIAL DIVISION]`
`HOLDEN AT [COURT]`

**Case No.:** `[CASE NUMBER]`

**Plaintiff:** `[PLAINTIFF NAME]`
**Defendant:** `[DEFENDANT NAME]`

**Document Title:** `STATEMENT OF CLAIM`

**Factual Background:**
1. The Claimant is [DESCRIPTION].
2. The Defendant is [DESCRIPTION].
3. On `[DATE]`, the Claimant and Defendant entered into a written agreement.
4. Under the agreement, the Defendant agreed to `[OBLIGATION]`.
5. The Claimant performed all obligations under the agreement.
6. The Defendant failed to `[PERFORM OBLIGATION]` despite demand.
7. As a result, the Claimant suffered damages of `[AMOUNT]`.

**Legal Claim:**
**Count One – Breach of Contract**
8. The Claimant incorporates paragraphs 1–7.
9. A valid contract existed between the parties.
10. The Claimant performed or was excused from performance.
11. The Defendant breached the contract.
12. The Claimant suffered damages as a result.
`[CITE SUPPORTING AUTHORITY]`

**Prayer for Relief:**
WHEREFORE, the Claimant claims against the Defendant for `[AMOUNT]`, costs of suit, and such other relief as the Court deems just.

**Signature Block:**
`[ATTORNEY NAME]`
`[BAR NUMBER]`
`[FIRM]`
`[ADDRESS]`
`[PHONE]`
`[EMAIL]`

## Limitations

- This skill does **not** know the latest local rules or emergency orders.
- It cannot verify facts or the current status of cited authorities.
- It does **not** replace legal research, strategy, or professional judgment.
- It should **not** be used to file documents without licensed attorney review.
- It is not a substitute for legal advice or representation.

---

## Output Format

Provide a clean draft in Markdown or plain text.
Include a short “Missing Information / Verification Required” section after the draft.
Always end with a reminder that a licensed attorney must review the document before filing.
