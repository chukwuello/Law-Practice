# Comprehensive Legal Drafting & AI Content Suite

A unified skill that combines AI content refinement, prompt engineering, image regeneration, and rigorous legal drafting—grounded in Nigerian law and practice. It draws on a curated repository of statutes (CAMA 2020, Sheriffs and Civil Process Act), educational materials on pleadings vs. evidence, and a library of sample legal drafts.

---

## Description

This skill enables you to:

- **Humanise AI-generated text** to sound natural and human-written.
- **Reverse-engineer prompts** from AI outputs to understand or replicate them.
- **Iteratively refine AI-generated images** through precise prompt engineering.
- **Draft legal pleadings and court documents** with a strict focus on material facts (never evidence), procedural compliance, and ethical safeguards.
- **Access and apply Nigerian legal statutes** (CAMA 2020, Sheriffs and Civil Process Act) and **sample drafts** for civil litigation, criminal litigation, property law, and company law.

It is designed for legal practitioners, law students, content creators, and anyone who needs to produce high-quality, jurisdiction-aware text or images while adhering to professional and ethical standards.

---

## Core Principles

1. **Facts are pleaded; evidence is proved.**  
   Pleadings contain **material facts only**—never the evidence by which they are to be proved. This principle is enforced in all legal drafting.

2. **No legal advice.**  
   This skill produces draft language and reference material only. It does **not** create an attorney–client relationship or replace independent legal judgment.

3. **Attorney review is mandatory.**  
   Every legal document must be reviewed by a licensed attorney before filing or reliance.

4. **Verify everything.**  
   All citations, statutes, and authorities must be independently checked. Never fabricate or guess citations.

5. **Comply with local rules.**  
   Always defer to jurisdiction-specific formatting, filing, and service requirements.

6. **Ethical guardrails.**  
   Do not input confidential client information without proper safeguards. Follow professional conduct and unauthorized practice of law (UPL) rules.

## Sub-Skills & Capabilities

### 1. AI Humaniser

**Purpose:** Transform robotic AI text into natural, engaging, human-sounding prose.

**Process:**
1. Analyse the source text for structure, tone, and key points.
2. Identify AI-isms (overly formal phrases, lack of contractions, repetitive transitions).
3. Rewrite with human-like language:
   - Use contractions (don’t, it’s, we’ll).
   - Vary sentence length and rhythm.
   - Replace jargon with plain English where appropriate.
   - Add conversational fillers sparingly.
4. Preserve factual accuracy and logical flow.
5. Read aloud to catch stiffness.

**Example:**
- **AI input:** “It is essential to consider the multifaceted implications of this policy.”
- **Humanised output:** “We need to think about how this policy will really affect things on the ground.”

### 2. Reverse Prompt Engineering

**Purpose:** Infer or reconstruct the original prompt(s) that generated a given AI output.

**Process:**
1. Examine the output’s length, structure, tone, and formatting.
2. Identify hard constraints (word count, list length, role-playing, prohibitions).
3. Parse the content architecture (sections, arguments, narrative).
4. Reconstruct a plausible prompt template covering instruction, context, style, and constraints.
5. Validate by feeding the inferred prompt to a new AI instance if possible.

**Example:**
- **Output:** A bulleted list of 5 professional advantages of remote work.
- **Inferred prompt:** “List 5 professional advantages of working remotely, in bullet points.”

---

### 3. Image Regeneration

**Purpose:** Refine AI-generated images through iterative prompt engineering.

**Process:**
1. Assess the initial image: composition, style, colours, subject details.
2. Identify flaws or missing elements (anatomy, lighting, background).
3. Craft a refined prompt with:
   - Specific subject and action descriptions.
   - Style modifiers (photorealistic, oil painting, cyberpunk, etc.).
   - Quality boosters (highly detailed, 8k, award-winning composition).
   - Negative prompts (deformed hands, blurry, oversaturated).
4. Regenerate, evaluate, and adjust until satisfied.

**Tips:**
| Element | Example |
|--------|---------|
| Subject | “a majestic lion with a full mane” |
| Action | “standing on a golden savanna at sunset” |
| Style | “in the style of Studio Ghibli, soft watercolour” |
| Lighting | “golden hour, dramatic shadows” |
| Quality | “hyper-realistic, intricate details, cinematic” |
| Negative | “no distortion, no blur, no extra limbs” |

### 4. Legal Practice Pleading

**Purpose:** Generate structured, jurisdiction-aware legal pleadings and court documents using a disciplined drafting methodology.

**Core Rule:** Pleadings contain **material facts only**—never the evidence by which they are to be proved.

**Process:**
1. **Case intake:** Confirm document type, jurisdiction, parties, facts, claims, reliefs.
2. **Jurisdiction check:** Confirm court, venue, procedural rules, deadlines.
3. **Legal research:** Identify statutes, regulations, case law; verify authority.
4. **Fact organisation:** Arrange facts chronologically; separate disputed from undisputed; do **not** include evidence.
5. **Outline and structure:** Use standard pleading structure (caption, parties, jurisdiction, facts, claims, prayer, signature).
6. **Draft with precision:**
   - Numbered paragraphs; one fact per paragraph.
   - Active voice; precise details (dates, amounts, names).
   - No evidence (receipts, witness statements, documents).
   - Cite authority for every legal proposition.
7. **Citation verification:** Check every citation against the original source; never fabricate.
8. **Compliance and formatting:** Apply local rules; include certificates and attachments.
9. **Review checklist:** Run through quality checklist; mark unresolved issues.
10. **Final output:** Provide clean draft with placeholders for missing info; include verification warnings; remind about attorney review.
