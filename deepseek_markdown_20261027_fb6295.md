DEED OF MORTGAGE

THIS DEED OF MORTGAGE is made the [DAY] day of [MONTH], [YEAR]

BETWEEN:
[MORTGAGOR NAME] of [ADDRESS] (the "Mortgagor") of the one part

AND

[MORTGAGEE NAME] of [ADDRESS] (the "Mortgagee") of the other part.

WHEREAS:
1. The Mortgagor is entitled to the property situate at [ADDRESS] which is covered by Certificate of Occupancy dated [DATE] with Registration No. [NUMBER].
2. The Mortgagee has agreed to lend to the Mortgagor the sum of [AMOUNT] ([AMOUNT IN WORDS]).
3. This mortgage is made subject to Governor's consent to be obtained by the Mortgagor.

THIS DEED WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:
The Mortgagor hereby mortgages all that property situate at [ADDRESS] which is covered by Certificate of Occupancy dated [DATE] with Registration No. [NUMBER] for a legal estate for the residue of the term of years granted by the Lease.

I, the Mortgagor hereby covenant with the Mortgagee to pay the Mortgagee on the [DAY] day of every month the sum of [AMOUNT] ([AMOUNT IN WORDS]) with interest thereon at the rate of [PERCENTAGE]% per annum.

The Mortgagor shall, not later than [DATE], pay to the Mortgagee in full the said sum outstanding on the interest and the principal sum under this deed to redeem his property.

THE MORTGAGOR COVENANTS WITH MORTGAGEE AS FOLLOWS:
1. The Mortgagor covenants that the property shall not be redeemable until at least [NUMBER] months after the execution of the contract.
2. The Mortgagor covenants to pay all sums expended in insuring the property.
3. The Mortgagor covenants to consolidate the redemption of any subsequent mortgage undertaken with the Mortgagee.
