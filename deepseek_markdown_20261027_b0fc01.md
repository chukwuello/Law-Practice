## Part A: Corporate Affairs Commission

### Establishment of the Commission (s.1)

The Corporate Affairs Commission is established as a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal.

### Functions of the Commission (s.8)

The Commission is responsible for:
- Registration, regulation, and supervision of companies, business names, and incorporated trustees.
- Maintaining a company's registry in each State.
- Investigating the affairs of companies where necessary.
- Ensuring compliance with CAMA and regulations.

## Part B: Companies

### Chapter 1: Incorporation

#### Right to Form a Company (s.18)
- Any two or more persons may form and incorporate a company.
- **One person may form and incorporate a private company.**

#### Types of Companies (s.21)
- Company limited by shares
- Company limited by guarantee
- Unlimited company

#### Private Company (s.22)
- Must be stated in the memorandum.
- May restrict transfer of shares.
- Total members shall not exceed 50 (excluding employees).
- Cannot invite the public to subscribe for shares or debentures.

#### Public Company (s.24)
- Any company other than a private company.

#### Company Limited by Guarantee (s.26)
- For promotion of commerce, art, science, religion, sports, culture, education, research, charity, or similar objects.
- Income and property applied solely towards promotion of objects.
- No distribution of profits to members.
- Requires authority of the Attorney-General of the Federation.

#### Memorandum of Association (s.27)
- Must state:
  - Name of company
  - Registered office in Nigeria
  - Nature of business/objects
  - Restriction on powers (if any)
  - Whether private or public
  - Liability of members
- Minimum issued share capital:
  - Private company: ₦100,000
  - Public company: ₦2,000,000

#### Name of Company (s.28–31)
- Private company: ends with "Limited" or "Ltd"
- Public company: ends with "Public Limited Company" or "PLC"
- Company limited by guarantee: ends with "Limited by Guarantee" or "Ltd/Gte"
- Unlimited company: ends with "Unlimited" or "Ultd"
- Name must be reserved with the Commission (valid for 60 days).

#### Registration Documents (s.36–40)
- Application for registration
- Statement of initial share capital and shareholdings
- Statement of guarantee (for guarantee companies)
- Statement of proposed directors
- Statement of compliance

#### Effect of Registration (s.42)
- Company becomes a body corporate with perpetual succession.
- Can sue and be sued.
- Can hold land.

### Chapter 2: Re-registration

- Private company → Public company (s.56)
- Public company → Private limited company (s.63)
- Private limited company → Unlimited company (s.68)
- Unlimited company → Limited company (s.71)
- Public company → Unlimited company (s.75)

---

### Chapter 3: Foreign Companies

#### Foreign Companies Intending to Carry on Business (s.78)
- Must incorporate as a separate entity in Nigeria.
- Until incorporated, cannot carry on business or have a place of business.

#### Exemption (s.80)
- Foreign companies may apply to the Minister for exemption if:
  - Invited by the Federal Government for a specific project.
  - Executing specific loan projects on behalf of a donor country or international organisation.
  - Government-owned, engaged solely in export promotion.
  - Engineering consultants/technical experts on a specific project.

---

### Chapter 4: Promoters

#### Definition (s.85)
A person who undertakes to take part in forming a company or raising capital for it.

#### Duties and Liabilities (s.86)
- Stands in a fiduciary relationship to the company.
- Must observe utmost good faith.
- Must account for any property or profit acquired as a fiduciary.

---

### Chapter 5: Acts by or on Behalf of the Company

#### Division of Powers (s.87)
- Company acts through members in general meeting, board of directors, or officers/agents.
- Business managed by board of directors.

#### Acts of General Meeting, Board, or Managing Director (s.89)
- Treated as acts of the company.
- Company is criminally and civilly liable.

#### Presumptions of Regularity (s.93)
- Persons dealing with a company may assume:
  - Memorandum and articles have been complied with.
  - Directors/secretary have been duly appointed.
  - Documents have been duly sealed.

#### Pre-incorporation Contracts (s.96)
- May be ratified by the company after formation.
- Prior to ratification, the person who purported to act is personally bound.

---

### Chapter 6: Membership

#### Definition of Member (s.105)
- Subscriber to the memorandum.
- Person who agrees in writing to become a member and is entered in the register.

#### Capacity to be a Member (s.106)
- Not capable: person of unsound mind, undischarged bankrupt, corporate body in liquidation.
- Minors (under 18) not counted for legal minimum.

#### Register of Members (s.109)
- Must contain names, addresses, shares held, date of registration, date of cessation.

#### Disclosure of Persons with Significant Control (s.119)
- Persons with significant control must notify the company within 7 days.
- Company must notify the Commission within 1 month.

---

### Chapter 7: Share Capital

#### Minimum Issued Share Capital (s.124)
- Private company: ₦100,000
- Public company: ₦2,000,000

#### Alteration of Share Capital (s.125)
- Consolidate and divide shares.
- Subdivide shares.

#### Increase of Issued Share Capital (s.127)
- By ordinary resolution.
- Notice to the Commission within 15 days.

#### Reduction of Share Capital (s.130–134)
- Requires special resolution and Court confirmation.

#### Duty of Directors on Serious Loss of Capital (s.137)
- If net assets are half or less of called-up share capital, directors must convene an extraordinary general meeting within 30 days.

---

### Chapter 8: Shares

#### Rights and Liabilities (s.138)
- Dependent on terms of issue or articles.
- Include right to attend and vote at general meetings.

#### Prohibition of Non-voting and Weighted Shares (s.140)
- Each share carries one vote on a poll.
- No company may authorise shares with more than one vote per share.

#### Issue of Shares (s.141–148)
- Power to issue shares.
- Pre-emptive rights of existing shareholders (s.142).
- Issue at a premium (s.145).
- Issue at a discount is unlawful (s.146).
- Redeemable preference shares (s.147).

#### Allotment of Shares (s.149–157)
- Authority to allot shares.
- Method of application and allotment.
- Return of allotment to the Commission within 1 month (s.154).

#### Calls on Shares (s.158–165)
- Directors may make calls.
- Lien on shares (s.164).
- Forfeiture of shares (s.165).

---

### Chapter 9: Debentures

#### Power to Borrow (s.191)
- Company may borrow money and issue debentures.

#### Types of Debentures (s.196–199)
- Perpetual debentures.
- Convertible debentures.
- Secured or unsecured debentures.
- Redeemable debentures.

#### Fixed and Floating Charges (s.203–207)
- Floating charge crystallises on enforcement, appointment of receiver, or liquidation.
- Fixed charge has priority over floating charge.

#### Registration of Charges (s.222–230)
- Charges must be registered with the Commission within 90 days.
- Register of charges kept by the Commission.

---

### Chapter 10: Meetings

#### Statutory Meeting (s.235)
- Every public company must hold within 6 months of incorporation.
- Directors must forward statutory report to members at least 21 days before.

#### Annual General Meeting (s.237)
- Every company (except small company or single shareholder) must hold each year.
- Not more than 15 months between meetings.

#### Extraordinary General Meeting (s.239)
- May be convened by directors or requisitioned by members holding at least 10% of paid-up capital.

#### Notice of Meeting (s.241–244)
- 21 days' notice required.
- Must specify place, date, time, and general nature of business.

#### Voting (s.248–255)
- Show of hands unless a poll is demanded.
- Proxies allowed.
- Corporation representation.

#### Quorum (s.256)
- One-third of total members or 25 members (whichever is less).

#### Resolutions (s.258–263)
- Ordinary resolution: simple majority.
- Special resolution: at least 75% of votes cast.

---

### Chapter 11: Directors

#### Meaning of Director (s.269)
- Person duly appointed by the company to direct and manage the business.

#### Shadow Director (s.270)
- Person on whose instructions and directions the directors are accustomed to act.

#### Number of Directors (s.271)
- Every company (not a small company) must have at least two directors.

#### Appointment of First Directors (s.272)
- Determined in writing by subscribers or named in the articles.

#### Independent Directors in Public Companies (s.275)
- At least three independent directors.

#### Disqualification (s.283)
- Infant (under 18).
- Person of unsound mind.
- Suspended or removed under s.288.
- Disqualified under ss.279, 280, 284.
- Corporation (other than its representative).

#### Vacation of Office (s.284)
- Ceases to be director under s.277.
- Becomes bankrupt.
- Prohibited by order.
- Becomes of unsound mind.
- Resigns by notice in writing.

#### Removal of Directors (s.288)
- By ordinary resolution.
- Special notice required.

#### Duties of Directors (s.305–308)
- Fiduciary relationship.
- Act in best interests of the company.
- Exercise powers for proper purpose.
- Duty of care and skill.
- Avoid conflicts of interest.
- No secret profits.

#### Multiple Directorships (s.307)
- Person shall not be a director in more than five public companies.

#### Personal Liability (s.316)
- Directors personally liable for refund of money/property received for specific purpose and not applied.

---

### Chapter 12: Secretaries

#### Requirement (s.330)
- Every company (except small company) must have a secretary.

#### Qualification (s.332)
- Member of ICSAN.
- Legal practitioner.
- Member of professional accountancy body.
- Person with at least 3 years' experience as secretary of a public company.
- Body corporate or firm of qualified members.

#### Duties (s.335)
- Attend meetings.
- Maintain registers and records.
- Render returns and notifications to the Commission.
- Administrative and secretarial duties.

---

### Chapter 13: Protection of Minority

#### Only Company May Sue (s.341)
- Where an irregularity is made, only the company can sue to remedy the wrong.

#### Protection of Minority (s.343)
- Court may grant injunction or declaration to restrain illegal or ultra vires acts.

#### Personal and Representative Action (s.344)
- Member may institute personal action or representative action.

#### Derivative Action (s.346–352)
- Member may apply to Court for leave to bring an action in the name of the company.

#### Relief on Grounds of Unfairly Prejudicial and Oppressive Conduct (s.353–356)
- Member, director, creditor, or Commission may petition the Court.
- Court may make orders including winding up, regulating conduct, purchase of shares, etc.

---

### Chapter 14: Financial Statements

#### Accounting Records (s.374)
- Must be kept to show and explain transactions.
- Disclose financial position with reasonable accuracy.

#### Place and Duration (s.375)
- Kept at registered office or other place in Nigeria.
- Preserved for 6 years.

#### Directors' Duty to Prepare Annual Accounts (s.377)
- Financial statements for each year.
- Include balance sheet, profit and loss account, notes, auditors' report, directors' report, cash flow statement, value-added statement, five-year summary.

#### Form and Content (s.378–382)
- Comply with First Schedule and accounting standards.
- True and fair view.

---

### Chapter 15: Audit

#### Appointment of Auditors (s.401)
- At each annual general meeting.
- Hold office until conclusion of next AGM.

#### Exemption from Audit (s.402)
- Small companies exempt.

#### Qualification (s.403)
- Not an officer or servant of the company.
- Not a partner or employee of an officer.
- Not a body corporate.

#### Auditors' Report (s.404)
- Report to members on accounts examined.
- Public companies must have an audit committee.

#### Corporate Responsibility for Financial Reports (s.405)
- CEO and CFO must certify audited financial statements.

---

### Chapter 16: Annual Returns

#### Annual Return (s.417–425)
- Every company must make and deliver to the Commission once at least in every year.
- Small companies have simplified requirements.
- Must be completed within 42 days after AGM.
- Failure to file for 10 consecutive years is grounds for striking off.

---

### Chapter 17: Company Voluntary Arrangements

#### Proposal (s.434)
- Directors may make a proposal to creditors for a composition in satisfaction of debts or scheme of arrangement.

#### Nominee (s.434)
- Person qualified to act as insolvency practitioner.

#### Meetings (s.436–437)
- Summoned to consider proposal.
- Decisions on approval.

---

### Chapter 18: Administration

#### Appointment of Administrator (s.443)
- By administration order of the Court.
- By holder of a floating charge.
- By the company or its directors.

#### Purpose of Administration (s.444)
- Rescue the company as a going concern.
- Achieve a better result for creditors.
- Realise property to make distribution to secured/preferential creditors.

#### Moratorium (s.480)
- No legal process against the company without consent of administrator or permission of Court.

---

### Chapter 19: Receivers and Managers

#### Appointment (s.550–553)
- By Court or out of Court under powers in an instrument.

#### Disqualification (s.550)
- Infant, person of unsound mind, body corporate, undischarged bankrupt, director or auditor of the company, person convicted of fraud.

#### Duties and Powers (s.556)
- Take possession of property.
- Protect property.
- Receive rents and profits.
- Realise security.

---

### Chapter 20: Winding Up

#### Modes of Winding Up (s.564)
- By the Court.
- Voluntarily.
- Subject to supervision of the Court.

#### Circumstances for Winding Up by Court (s.571)
- Special resolution.
- Default in delivering statutory report or holding statutory meeting.
- Number of members reduced below two.
- Unable to pay debts.
- Condition precedent to operation ceased.
- Just and equitable.

#### Inability to Pay Debts (s.572)
- Creditor owed more than ₦200,000 serves demand and company neglects for 3 weeks.
- Execution returned unsatisfied.
- Court satisfied company unable to pay debts.

#### Voluntary Winding Up (s.620)
- Members' voluntary winding up (with declaration of solvency).
- Creditors' voluntary winding up.

#### Preferential Payments (s.657)
- Local rates and charges.
- PAYE tax deductions.
- Pension Reform Act contributions.
- Employees' Compensation Act contributions.
- Wages or salaries.
- Accrued holiday remuneration.

---

## Part C: Limited Liability Partnerships

### Nature (s.746)
- Body corporate with legal personality separate from partners.
- Perpetual succession.

### Partners (s.747–748)
- At least two partners.
- Individual or body corporate.

### Designated Partners (s.749)
- At least two designated partners, at least one resident in Nigeria.

### Incorporation (s.753–754)
- Incorporation document filed with the Commission.
- Certificate of incorporation issued.

### Liability (s.766–769)
- LLP liable for its obligations.
- Partner not personally liable solely by reason of being a partner.
- Unlimited liability in case of fraud.

### Winding Up (s.789–790)
- Voluntary or by Court.

---

## Part D: Limited Partnerships

### Constitution (s.795)
- One or more general partners (liable for all debts).
- One or more limited partners (liable up to contributed amount).
- Not more than 20 persons.

### Registration (s.797–799)
- Must be registered with the Commission.
- Application includes name, business, partners, contributions.

### Management (s.806)
- Limited partner shall not take part in management.
- If he does, he is liable for debts as a general partner.

---

## Part E: Business Names

### Registration (s.814–817)
- Every individual, firm, or corporation carrying on business under a business name must register.
- Within 28 days of commencing business.
- Statement of particulars furnished to the Registrar.

### Certificate of Registration (s.817)
- Issued by the Registrar.
- Must be exhibited at the principal place of business.

### Removal from Register (s.819)
- If business ceases, notice must be sent within 3 months.

### Annual Returns (s.822)
- Not later than 30th June each year.

---

## Part F: Incorporated Trustees

### Incorporation (s.823–830)
- Two or more trustees appointed by a community or association.
- Apply to the Commission for registration.
- Certificate of incorporation issued.

### Constitution (s.827)
- Name, aims and objects, appointment of trustees, use of common seal, meetings, governing body, sources of income, disbursement of funds, accounts, auditing.

### Changes (s.832–835)
- Change of name or objects requires application to the Commission.
- Replacement or appointment of additional trustees.

### Dissolution (s.850)
- By Court on petition by governing body, trustees, members, or Commission.

---

## Part G: General

### Administrative Proceedings Committee (s.851)
- Established by the Commission.
- Provides opportunity of being heard.
- Resolves disputes.
- Imposes administrative penalties.

### Prohibited and Restricted Names (s.852)
- Names identical or nearly resembling existing names.
- Names containing "Chamber of Commerce" (unless company limited by guarantee).
- Names capable of misleading.
- Names violating trademarks.
- Names suggesting government patronage.
- Names containing "Cooperative", "Building Society", "Group", or "Holding".

### Miscellaneous (s.859–870)
- Resubmission of lost or destroyed documents.
- Electronic documents.
- Preservation of documents.
- Penalty for false statements.
- Penalty for carrying on business without registration.
- Access to premises.
- Power to compound offences.
- Regulations.
- Interpretation.
- Repeal and savings.
- Citation.
