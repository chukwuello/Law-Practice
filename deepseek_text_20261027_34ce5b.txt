
**Examples – Facts vs. Evidence:**

| ❌ Incorrect | ✅ Correct |
|---|---|
| “The Claimant paid ₦500,000 to the Defendant as evidenced by the receipt attached hereto.” | “The Claimant paid ₦500,000 to the Defendant on 1st January 2024.” |
| “The Defendant admitted liability in a WhatsApp message dated 2nd February.” | “On 2nd February 2024, the Defendant admitted liability.” |

**Key Authorities:**
- **Aghadiuno v. Onubogu (1998) 5 NWLR (Pt. 548) 16** – Averments in pleadings are not evidence.
- **Ijomah v. Molokwu (2025) 21 NWLR (Pt. 2023) 145** – Evidence at variance with pleadings is inadmissible.
- **FCT High Court Civil Procedure Rules, Order 15 Rule 2** – Pleadings must state material facts, **not the evidence** by which they are to be proved.
