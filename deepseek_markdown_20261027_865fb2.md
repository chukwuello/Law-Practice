# Sample Drafts and Precedents

A curated collection of legal forms, precedents, and drafting templates for Nigerian legal practice, organised by practice area.

---

## How to Use These Precedents

1. **Select the relevant practice area** from the table below.
2. **Adapt the template** to the facts of your case, replacing all placeholders (`[DATE]`, `[NAME]`, `[AMOUNT]`, etc.).
3. **Verify jurisdiction-specific rules** (court formats, filing requirements, deadlines).
4. **Review and finalise** with a licensed attorney before filing.

## Important Notes

- **All templates are illustrative.** They must be adapted to the specific facts and circumstances of each case.
- **Local rules vary.** Always check the rules of the court or jurisdiction where the document will be filed.
- **Statutory references** are to Nigerian law (CAMA 2020, Sheriffs and Civil Process Act, etc.) unless otherwise indicated.
- **Attorney review is required** before filing or relying on any document.
