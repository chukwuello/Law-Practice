THIS POWER OF ATTORNEY is given this [DAY] day of [MONTH], [YEAR] by [DONOR NAME] of [ADDRESS] (the "Donor") TO [DONEE NAME] of [ADDRESS] (the "Donee").

WHEREAS:
1. The Donor is seized of all that piece or parcel of land situate at [ADDRESS].
2. The Donor is desirous of appointing the Donee as his Attorney in respect of all his interest in the property.
3. This Power of Attorney is irrevocable.

NOW THIS DEED WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:
