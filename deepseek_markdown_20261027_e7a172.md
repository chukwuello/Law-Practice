## Commencement of Action

### Writ of Summons (High Court)

```markdown
IN THE HIGH COURT OF [STATE]
IN THE [JUDICIAL DIVISION]
HOLDEN AT [COURT]

SUIT NO: [SUIT NUMBER]

BETWEEN:
[PLAINTIFF NAME] ..................................... CLAIMANT

AND

[DEFENDANT NAME] .................................. DEFENDANT

TO: [DEFENDANT NAME]
[ADDRESS]

You are hereby commanded that within [NUMBER] days after the service of this writ on you, inclusive of the day of such service, you do cause an appearance to be entered for you in an action at the suit of the Claimant and take notice that in default of your so doing the Claimant may proceed therein, and judgment may be given in your absence.

DATED this [DAY] day of [MONTH], [YEAR].

[N.B.: This writ is to be served within six calendar months from the date thereof...]

ENDORSEMENT:
The Claimant claims against the Defendant:
1. [CLAIM 1]
2. [CLAIM 2]
3. [CLAIM 3]

This writ was issued by [SOLICITOR NAME], [FIRM], [ADDRESS], Claimant's Solicitors.
